# VeilVPN — Acceptable Use Policy

**Effective:** October 9, 2026

This Policy is part of the [Terms of Service](TERMS.md). "Service" means
the VeilVPN apps, servers, API, and website.

## 1. The short version

Use VeilVPN lawfully and don't ruin it for everyone else. Because we are a
no-logs service, we cannot see what you do — but abuse reports, fraud
signals, and account-level patterns can still lead to suspension.

## 2. Prohibited activities

You must not use the Service to:

- **Break the law** — including fraud, theft, harassment, threats, or
  distributing illegal content such as CSAM.
- **Infringe intellectual property** — including distributing or downloading
  copyrighted material without authorization.
- **Attack systems** — hacking, unauthorized access, port scanning of third
  parties, DDoS, or distributing malware, ransomware, or phishing.
- **Spam or abuse** — sending unsolicited bulk messages, operating spam
  infrastructure, or scraping in violation of target sites' terms at
  abusive volumes.
- **Abuse the Service itself** — reselling access without permission,
  sharing one account across many people, chaining free trials with
  multiple accounts, circumventing device limits or billing, or interfering
  with other users' service.
- **Evade enforcement** — creating new accounts to dodge a suspension or ban.

## 3. Network conduct

- Sustained, extreme bandwidth use far beyond typical personal use may be
  rate-limited to protect the network for everyone (see Terms §6).
- Do not run public-facing servers, exit relays, or Tor exit nodes over the
  Service without prior written permission.

## 4. Reporting abuse

If you believe someone is misusing VeilVPN, contact us via the abuse
contact on the website's contact page with:

- A description of the abuse and when it occurred (UTC);
- Any evidence you can share (e.g. log excerpts, headers, URLs — never
  other people's private data);
- Your contact details for follow-up.

Valid reports are recorded as abuse flags (evidence reference + decision
metadata only — no traffic data, which we do not have).

## 5. Enforcement

Depending on severity, we may:

- Warn you and ask you to stop;
- Temporarily suspend VPN access;
- Permanently terminate the account (no refund for termination for cause);
- Report illegal activity to law enforcement where required by law.

Enforcement actions are recorded in our hash-chained admin audit log.

## 6. Changes

We will post changes here with an updated effective date and give at least
14 days' notice of material changes.
